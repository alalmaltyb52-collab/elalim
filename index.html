01 — ABOUT

A clinician with a researcher's mindset.

I am a medical doctor, researcher and medical educator interested in turning scientific evidence into better learning, better decisions and better health outcomes.

I graduated with an MBBS from the Faculty of Medicine, Red Sea University, Sudan, with First Class Honors in May 2026. My academic work spans medical education, healthcare research, quality improvement, scientific writing and evidence synthesis.

My longer-term direction brings together clinical medicine, neuroscience/neurosurgery, research and education — with a strong interest in collaborative, internationally relevant academic work.
